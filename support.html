Support

Help for Yamato Match on iPhone and iPad.

Need assistance? Email hobby-happymylife-info@hobby-happymylife.com. Please include your app version, device model, and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment card details.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase. If it still does not appear, contact us with the purchase date; do not email your card number.

How do I switch culture lessons between English and Japanese?

Open a culture lesson from Explore Japan or the Learning Path, then use the English / Japanese control. Switching languages keeps your quiz progress.

Where are my personal words and progress?

My Words cards and learning progress are saved on this device. Check that you are using the same device and have not deleted the app. There is no account-based sync.

Why do I see ads or why is an ad missing?

Supported free screens may show Google AdMob ads. Availability depends on your region, network connection, consent choices, and available ads. You can still use the core learning features when an ad is unavailable.

How do I review advertising privacy choices?

When required in your region, Settings shows Ad privacy options. You can also use your device's privacy controls. See our Privacy Policy for the data categories associated with AdMob.

Pronunciation does not play.

Check your device volume and audio output, then try the phrase again. If the issue continues, include the affected lesson in your email.
