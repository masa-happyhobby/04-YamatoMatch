Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Yamato Match is a Japanese language and culture learning app. This policy explains how the app and its third-party services handle data.

Learning data on your device

No account or registration is required. Learning progress, personal word cards, language preferences, and theme choices are stored on your device. The app uses on-device text-to-speech for pronunciation. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, unless your device backup retains a copy.

Advertising and data collection

Supported free screens may show ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads and consent SDKs may collect or process the following data when the app is used:

  • Approximate location, which may be inferred from an IP address.
  • Device identifiers, such as advertising or app-scoped identifiers.
  • Product interactions, such as app and ad interactions.
  • Advertising data, such as ads shown to you.
  • Crash, performance, and other diagnostic data.

These data may be used to serve and measure ads, analyze usage and performance, and support app functionality. Approximate location, device identifiers, product interactions, and advertising data may be linked to you. Diagnostic data are not linked to you in the app's App Store privacy disclosure. Device identifiers may be used for tracking across other companies' apps and websites for advertising where permitted.

The app uses Google's consent flow where required before requesting ads. Where available, you can review your advertising privacy options in the app's Settings. Device-level privacy controls also apply. For details about Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and its iOS AdMob data disclosure.

In-app purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. You can restore eligible purchases in the app.

Your choices and contact

You can remove locally stored learning data by deleting the app. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact hobby-happymylife-info@hobby-happymylife.com. For data handled by Google, consult Google's policy and privacy controls.
